A Class in Web Design

Hashtag CyberStar was a third-year class for me, which focused briefly on the history of the internet before shifting into website design, in both its design presentation and coding aspects. This was quite different from the maths and physics-based classes I’m used to taking, but after reading the class description I wanted to do two things: push myself to do something different and learn something along the way, and, for my final project, make a website I’d be able to keep and manage well after I’ve completed the class.

And here it is: this website, the one you are currently on, is my final project for this class. It’s most likely changed since its original iterations, but the premise, meaning and part of me it displays should still be the same. Click the design document’s cover to view the full thing.
