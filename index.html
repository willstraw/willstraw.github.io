CV

To properly introduce myself, I’m Will. I’m a current undergraduate student studying mechanical engineering, and for as long as I can remember (as cliché as it sounds) I’ve wanted to be an engineer. Conceptualisation, problem solving, design, iteration, testing, manufacturing, every step along the way I live and breathe for. I’m asked quite often what I study at university, and when I answer engineering, I always add that I couldn’t do anything else: not in a literal sense, but in that I couldn’t see myself doing anything but this. I love motorsport and have been surrounded by cars my entire life. Growing up in Melbourne, being taken out of school to go to the Grand Prix as a kid gave me some of my fondest memories. Now, getting the opportunity not only to work on but to design, and see come to life, parts for a real formula-style car is a dream come true, and just the beginning. A dream of mine would be to work in F1 someday, and this website is just one of the things along the way that I hope will help get me there. Have a look around, and I hope you enjoy it!
