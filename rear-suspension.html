As a vehicle dynamics engineer in my first year, I was tasked with designing the rear suspension around geometric hard points set at the start of the year. This was completely new to me: there was no walkthrough on how to do it, and no answers to check halfway through. As a fresh engineer I was thrown in and expected to swim. So I started researching: what were the parts I was designing (if I didn’t know), what do they do, why do we have them, and how do others design them?

After a lengthy research period, I realised that I also had my peers. All these parts had been designed the year before for a different car, and the people with that experience were right around me. This is when I really felt like I was becoming a part of the team, when I finally started collaborating with those around me.

There were many mishaps along the way and the design sure isn’t perfect, but what I do know is that if I did it again I could definitely do it better, and if I did something completely different, I know I’d improve on many of my original approaches.
